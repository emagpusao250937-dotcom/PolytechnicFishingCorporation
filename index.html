About Polytechnic Fishing Corporation

Quality seafood products supported by organized logistics and modern technology.

Our Company

Polytechnic Fishing Corporation (PFC) is a seafood company based in Navotas. We provide tuna products that are carefully sourced, processed, stored, and delivered to customers and retailers.

Our Mission

To provide quality and affordable fish products while maintaining freshness, safety, and good service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted seafood company known for quality, innovation, and reliable service.
